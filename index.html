Most swap tools on a new chain make you wrap BOT in one place, approve in another and hope the price hasn't moved by the time you sign. BotFlow puts all of it in one flow and tells you plainly what each trade will cost.

What BotFlow does for you

Smart routing

Every trade is quoted on direct pools at all four BDEX fee tiers and on two-hop routes through WBOT and USDT. You get the route that returns the most. Tokens without a direct pool can still be traded.

XYZ0.3%WBOT0.05%USDT

Native BOT, in and out

Pay with BOT or receive BOT. Wrapping and unwrapping happen inside the trade, and BOT to WBOT is a direct 1:1 wrap with no pool fee.

Know the cost first

Rate, route, price impact, minimum received and network fee are shown before you sign. High price impact turns the button red.

Any token, carefully

Paste any token address to trade it. BotFlow reads it from the chain and asks you to check it on the explorer before importing.

Links that open a trade

Share a link with the pair and amount filled in. Projects can send their community straight to a ready trade.

How a swap works

BotFlow only asks your wallet for the steps your trade actually needs.

  1. Quote

    The best route is found across every pool and refreshed every 15 seconds.

  2. Prepare

    BOT is wrapped and the token approved, but only if needed. Approvals are for the exact amount by default.

  3. Check

    The route is quoted again and the swap simulated. If the price fell below your minimum, nothing is sent.

  4. Swap

    You sign once. If you asked for BOT, it is unwrapped and sent to you.

Your keys, your funds

BotFlow never holds your tokens. Every transaction is signed in your own wallet, and you stay in control of what the router can spend.

Approvals panel
See every token the router can spend from your wallet and revoke any of them in one click.
Slippage and deadline
Set your own limits. Very low or very high values come with a warning.
Gas reserve
Swapping BOT always leaves 0.01 BOT in your wallet so you can pay network fees.
Plain errors
When something fails, you're told what happened and what to do next.

Built in the open

BotFlowRouter is a Solidity contract that sits in front of BDEX V3. It handles native BOT in one transaction, enforces your minimum on what you actually receive, supports fee-on-transfer tokens, and never keeps funds or approvals between calls.

Questions

Is this real money?

No. BotFlow currently runs on BOT Chain Testnet, where tBOT and test USDT have no value. You can get them free from the BOT Chain faucet, up to 10 tBOT and 1000 test USDT a day.

Does BotFlow hold my funds?

No. Every trade is signed in your own wallet and goes straight to the BOT Chain contracts. BotFlow has no backend and never has access to your keys.

What does a swap cost?

BotFlow charges no fee. You pay the BDEX pool fee for the route (0.01% to 1%, shown before you sign) and the BOT Chain network fee.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM wallet, including MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Bitget Wallet and TokenPocket, on desktop or in a wallet's built-in browser. BotFlow adds BOT Chain Testnet to your wallet if it isn't there yet.

Is the contract audited?

Not yet. BotFlowRouter has a full test suite and is open source for anyone to review. An independent security review is on the roadmap before it handles user trades.

Is BotFlow part of BOT Chain?

No. BotFlow is an independent, open source project built on BOT Chain and BDEX liquidity.
